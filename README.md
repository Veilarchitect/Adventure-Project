## 📜 Overview

**The Choice: Path of Integrity** places you in the shoes of a newly appointed public servant facing a series of challenging ethical dilemmas. This interactive Python game, developed as part of the **DECi-LV2 program**, forces you to confront difficult decisions where the lines between right and wrong blur.

Your choices have tangible consequences, directly shaping your:

- 🌟 **Integrity Score:** Your core moral compass. Reach 100 to achieve true success, but fall to 0, and your career ends in disgrace.
- 🤝 **Reputation:** How you're perceived by colleagues, superiors, and the public.
- 😥 **Stress Levels:** Manage pressure, as high stress impacts judgment and performance.
- 👤 **Relationships:** Cultivate connections with Mentors, Allies, and navigate challenging interactions with Rivals.

Featuring **12 immersive scenarios** with branching narratives, multiple endings, and a dynamic stat-tracking system, **The Choice** offers a rich environment for exploring the complexities of ethical decision-making.

2. **Git:** Make sure Git is installed on your system. Get it from [git-scm.com](https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2/Getting-Started-Installing-Git).

## ▶️ How to Play

1. **Launch the Game:** Run `python Adventure_game.py` from the project directory.
2. **Read Carefully:** Each scenario will be presented with narrative text, mimicking a typewriter effect.
3. **Make Your Choice:** You'll be given multiple options (usually 1-4). Type the number corresponding to your chosen action and press Enter.
4. **Observe Consequences:** After each decision, the game will update your stats (Integrity, Reputation, Stress) and potentially award Wisdom, Inspiration, or Caution points. Pay close attention to these changes!
5. **Navigate the Narrative:** Progress through the 12 scenarios, shaping your story.
6. **Goal:** Aim to maximize your **Integrity** score (reach 100 for the ideal ending) while managing **Reputation** and **Stress** effectively. Avoid letting Integrity drop to 0!

## 🗺️ Scenarios

Prepare to face a gauntlet of ethical challenges:

- **Corruption Case:** Uncover a suspicious deal. *Do you blow the whistle?*
- **Bribe Offer:** A tempting envelope lands on your desk. *What's your price?*
- **Whistleblower Dilemma:** Witness misconduct by a colleague. *Report or stay silent?*
- **Resource Allocation:** Distribute limited funds fairly. *Whose needs come first?*
- **Nepotism Pressure:** A superior favors an unqualified relative. *Do you challenge them?*
- **Policy Influence:** Lobbyists offer perks for favorable decisions. *Can you stay impartial?*
- **Misuse of Resources:** Observe minor rule-bending. *Where do you draw the line?*
- **Conflict of Interest:** Personal ties clash with professional duties. *How do you proceed?*
- **Public Statements:** Balance truth with political sensitivity. *What message do you send?*
- **Data Privacy:** Discover a security flaw. *Prioritize speed or security?*
- **Regulations Bypass:** Pressure mounts to cut corners. *Risk versus reward?*
- **Promotion Bias:** Suspect favoritism in advancements. *Speak up or play along?*

## ⚙️ Game Mechanics Explained

| Stat          | Range  | Impact                                                                 | Win/Loss Condition         |
|---------------|--------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Integrity** | 0-100  | Your moral core. Influences choices, dialogue, and unlocks specific narrative paths & endings. | **Win:** Reach 100         |
|               |        |                                                                        | **Lose:** Falls to 0       |
| **Reputation**| 0-100  | Affects how others perceive and interact with you. High reputation builds trust and opens doors. | -                          |
| **Stress**    | 0-100  | High stress can impair judgment, leading to mistakes or limiting available choices. | -                          |

**Additional Performance Metrics:**

- 💡 **Wisdom:** Gained from making thoughtful, ethical, and effective choices. Reflects learning and good judgment.
- ✨ **Inspiration:** Earned when your actions lead to positive outcomes or uphold strong ethical principles, motivating yourself and potentially others.
- ⚠️ **Caution:** Developed from experiencing negative consequences or navigating risky situations. Reflects learning from mistakes and prudence.

*(These metrics primarily serve as feedback on your playstyle and contribute to the narrative flavour.)*
